CrossRef metadata is reliable for DOI, title, journal, year and volume from established publishers; author given names are often initials only, online-first articles may lack pages, and titles are sometimes deposited in capitals. Title search is fuzzy: confirm the DOI matches the paper you mean. GOST 7.1-2003 is the version adopted in Kazakhstan (СТ РК ГОСТ 7.1-2003); journals apply house variants, so glance at the author guidelines. Sources without a DOI need manual entry.
